# SIH Demo Script — 5 minutes

## 0:00 — Problem

Victims can face distress not only at complaint registration, but throughout investigation, trial, compensation and rehabilitation. SaathiCare creates a continuous wellbeing signal and routes early warnings to human support.

## 0:30 — Victim experience

Open the PWA → demo survivor → Check-in.

Answer elevated values for anxiety, sleep, safety, threat and court stress.

## 1:30 — AI result

Show:
- Dynamic score
- Personal baseline
- Change from baseline
- Trend
- Explainable contributors
- Human-review recommendations

Say:
> “This is a screening estimate, not a diagnosis. The key innovation is that the score is longitudinal and contextual.”

## 2:15 — Case journey

Show:
Complaint → Investigation → Trial → Compensation → Rehabilitation.

Point out that wellbeing signals can be viewed alongside case milestones.

## 2:45 — Authority portal

Open the counsellor portal → Alerts.

Show the early warning.

Open the case.

Show:
- risk trend
- factors
- court event
- compensation
- rehabilitation

## 3:45 — Human intervention

Record an intervention and schedule a follow-up.

Say:
> “The AI prioritizes and explains; it does not make the sensitive decision.”

## 4:15 — Scale

Quickly show:
Voice AI, SMS/IVRS, Saathi AI, District, State, National.

Say:
> “The current prototype proves the closed loop. These additional channels are designed to feed the same longitudinal case record.”

## 4:45 — Close

> “SaathiCare moves the system from one-time legal/financial assistance toward continuous, explainable, human-supervised victim wellbeing support.”
